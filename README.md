# Project Overview & Analyst Role
This repository contains the work developed as part of the Data Analyst Department at **Banco Atlas**, within a simulation environment that replicates real banking operations, data flows, and analytical responsibilities. The project integrates business objectives, data workflows, and the full technological stack used by analysts to generate insights and support strategic decision‑making.

## About Banco Atlas
Banco Atlas is a financial institution operating across Spain, offering a wide range of products and services, including:
- Deposits
- Mortgage loans and housing financing
- Personal loans, SME loans, and credit lines

**Mission:** Provide reliable and accessible financial solutions that help customers grow, protect their assets, and achieve personal and business goals through innovation and responsibility.

## Data Flow and Operational Context
The simulator reproduces the real data lifecycle inside Banco Atlas.

### Data Origin
Customer interactions generate data related to:
- Personal and demographic information
- Financial activity
- Deposits, loans, and housing applications

## Business Roles & Analytical Objectives
The simulator incorporates three core analytical domains:

### Marketing & Communication Analysts
- Increase customer acquisition, conversion, and retention
- Analyze campaign performance
- Segment audiences
- Optimize communication channels

### Finance & Credit Risk Analysts
- Reduce financial risk
- Evaluate customer solvency
- Anticipate defaults
- Improve credit‑granting models

### Customer Profile Analysts
- Improve personalization of products and services
- Understand customer characteristics, needs, and behaviors

## Technological Environment
The project uses the same tools employed by Banco Atlas analysts:
- **Python** for data processing and analysis
- **MySQL Workbench** for database access and queries
- **Power BI** for dashboards and weekly presentations
- **Git & GitHub** for version control and collaboration

Weekly results are presented in a **single PDF file**.

## Project Structure
/data          → Raw and processed datasets (simulated)

/desafiaments  → Weekly challenges and business inquiries

/recursos      → Key information and educational guidelines

/results       → Weekly presentations

/scripts       → Python notebooks and scripts for analysis

## My Role in the Project
As a Data Analyst within the Banco Atlas simulation, my responsibilities include:
- Analyzing customer, financial, and operational data from multiple branches
- Preparing weekly insights and business proposals for the department director
- Prioritizing recommendations based on business impact and feasibility
- Identifying required resources and cross‑department collaboration
- Applying agile methodologies (Scrum, Kanban) to structure work cycles
- Using Git/GitHub for version control and collaborative workflows
- Creating dashboards and visual reports in Power BI

My work focuses on transforming data into actionable strategies that support decision‑making across the bank.

## Agile Work Framework
The project follows agile principles:
- Short iterative cycles
- Continuous delivery of value
- Close collaboration with the team
- Daily check‑ins and frequent reviews
- Kanban boards to visualize workflow and limit WIP
